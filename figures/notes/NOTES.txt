Uncertainties to account for here: 
1. sampling of ensemble members and timesteps -- bootstrapping 
2. number of bins used to estimate mutual information (MI) -- 1.5x bins - 0.5 bins 

For a lambda ratio calculation, we say that the lambda ratio computed at a given number of bins is meaningful 
only if the binning uncertainty is at most the lambda ratio itself. Otherwise, the effect of binning is too 
large for a reliable estimate of the true MI. 

If a lambda ratio is meaningful, then, we additionally test the sampling distribution generated by the boots-
trapping procedure. If the distribution excludes 1 from its 95% confidence interval, we say that the grid cell
does not have significant deviation from the expectation that RPC=1. The exact same procedure is applied to the 
rho ratios.
